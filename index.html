📘 Know Your Legal Rights

📚 Education Laws

  • Right to Education Act (RTE) 2009: Free & compulsory education for 6–14 years.
  • Every child has the right to a neighborhood school within 1 km.
  • Private schools must reserve 25% seats for disadvantaged children.
  • Corporal punishment is banned.
  • No detention policy up to Class 8.
  • Mid-day meals are compulsory in government schools.
  • Students can’t be denied admission for lack of documents.
  • Schools must provide complaint redressal mechanisms.
  • All institutions must be recognized and follow quality standards.

🏡 Property Laws

  • Equal Inheritance: All legal heirs, regardless of gender, have equal rights.
  • Encroachment is illegal under IPC Section 441.
  • Adverse possession after 12+ years can establish claim.
  • Benami Transactions are criminally punishable.
  • Double sale of property = Fraudulent under Indian Contract Act.
  • Gift deed must be legally registered.
  • Legal heirs can demand partition legally via court.
  • Registration & stamp duty are mandatory for sale transfers.

🚦 Traffic Laws

  • Driving without license: ₹5000 fine.
  • No helmet or seatbelt: ₹1000 fine.
  • Triple riding: ₹2000 fine.
  • Drunk driving: ₹10000 fine + jail.
  • Speed racing: ₹5000 fine.
  • Driving without insurance: ₹2000 fine.
  • Wrong side: ₹5000 fine.
  • Blocking ambulances: ₹10000 fine.
  • Overloaded vehicle: ₹20000+ fine.

💰 Anti-Corruption Laws

  • Prevention of Corruption Act 1988: Bribery, fraud, and misuse of power are criminal acts.
  • Offering bribes is punishable too.
  • Govt. officials must declare assets annually.
  • Whistleblowers are protected under special acts.
  • Complaints can be submitted to Lokayukta, ACB, or CVC.
  • Digital complaint portals are available (CVC portal).
  • Fast-track courts for corruption exist.
  • RTI empowers public to request info from govt departments.

🛡️ Cyber Laws

  • IT Act 2000: Protects against hacking, identity theft, and cyber fraud.
  • Cyberbullying and online harassment are punishable offenses.
  • Sending obscene messages online: punishable under Section 66A (Struck down but referenced).
  • Phishing and fake websites: covered under Section 66D.
  • Cyberstalking is a criminal offense under Section 354D of IPC.
  • Publishing private content without consent is punishable.
  • Data protection and online financial frauds fall under cyber law jurisdiction.

🧒 Child Protection Laws

  • POCSO Act 2012: Ensures protection from sexual offenses against children.
  • Child Labour (Prohibition) Act bans hazardous work for under 14 years.
  • Every child has a right to health, education, and nutrition.
  • Child marriage is illegal under the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act.
  • Abandoning or abusing children is punishable under Section 75 of JJ Act.
  • Helpline 1098 is a 24x7 service for child emergencies.

🧑‍⚖️ Fundamental Rights

  • Article 14: Equality before law and equal protection of the law.
  • Article 19: Freedom of speech, expression, and association.
  • Article 21: Right to life and personal liberty.
  • Article 25: Freedom of religion and conscience.
  • Article 32: Right to constitutional remedies (approach Supreme Court).
  • No person shall be deprived of property except by law (Article 300A).

🏢 Consumer Rights

  • Right to Safety: Protection against hazardous goods/services.
  • Right to Information: Know details of the product (price, expiry, ingredients).
  • Right to Choose: Access to a variety of goods and services at competitive prices.
  • Right to Redressal: Compensation for unfair trade practices or exploitation.
  • Consumer Protection Act 2019: Strengthened rights and redressal mechanism.
  • You can file complaints online at consumerhelpline.gov.in.
