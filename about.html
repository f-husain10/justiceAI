About JusticeAI

JusticeAI is an AI-powered legal awareness and assistance platform designed to help citizens understand laws, cyber safety, legal rights and legal procedures through smart digital solutions.

Project Objective

⚖️

Legal Awareness

Provide easy-to-understand legal education and citizen rights awareness using AI technology.

🤖

AI Assistance

Offer AI-powered legal guidance for cybercrime, FIR drafting, legal rights and awareness.

🛡️

Cyber Safety

Educate users about cyber fraud, OTP scams, phishing attacks and online financial security.

Technology Stack

JusticeAI is developed using modern frontend technologies and AI-integrated concepts for responsive user experience.
