Cybercrime Help Center

Get emergency guidance, fraud prevention tips and legal help for cybercrime incidents including UPI scams, OTP fraud and identity theft.

🚨 Emergency Cyber Fraud Helpline

If you lost money through online fraud, immediately call 1930 and report the incident to the National Cyber Crime Portal. Quick reporting increases recovery chances.

📱 UPI Scam Protection

Never share OTP, UPI PIN or remote access permissions with anyone.

🔒 Account Security

Enable two-factor authentication and regularly update passwords.

⚖️ Legal Rights

Victims can file FIRs and seek legal action under the IT Act provisions.
