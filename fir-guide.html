FIR Filing Guide

Understand the proper FIR filing process, required documents and citizen legal rights with this step-by-step AI legal awareness guide.

1. Visit Police Station

Visit the nearest police station having jurisdiction over the incident.

2. Describe the Incident

Clearly explain the incident, including date, time, location and involved persons.

3. Submit Evidence

Provide screenshots, documents, transaction details or proof supporting the complaint.

4. Obtain FIR Copy

Always request and keep a copy of the FIR for future legal reference.

📄 Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card / ID Proof
  • Incident Screenshots
  • Bank Statements (if needed)
  • Transaction Details
  • Witness Information
